What is real. The counts: 114 passages, 54 with a recording, 14 of 17 aspects, three true zeros. The navigation: a bottom bar with Notebook · Explore · Talk · Settings, Talk in the middle. The behaviour: a question can be asked, deferred or retired, and the draw varies; a thread or a story can be promoted into the next call; a photo creates a story rather than joining a tray.

What is fixture. Every sentence in quotation marks is the wireframe's invented family, not David's recording. The aspect names, story titles and counts follow his real record.
