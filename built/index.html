What is real here. The counts: 114 passages, 54 with a recording, 14 of 17 aspects, three true zeros (the shop, your children, beliefs). The navigation: Talk is the first item in the row on every screen, Home carries the plan, Archive carries every passage, and the place you are standing in is text rather than a control. Tap-through: a quotation opens its story, a story's play control plays its own seconds, a word can be corrected with the original kept.

What is fixture. Every sentence in quotation marks is the wireframe's invented family, not David's recording. The aspect names and story cards are drawn from his real themes.

What has no design in it yet. Photos: the queue says "not measured yet" because the media route does not exist. Notebook is gone as a place — its plan is the "What to ask next" section on Home.
